HELP & INQUIRIES

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about OPD tokens, cashless hospitalization, and patient data rights.

How does the Real-Time OPD Token system minimize my clinic wait time? +

When you reserve a slot online or at our reception kiosk, a unique Token ID (e.g., TK-108) is issued. You can track live chamber status on your smartphone. Instead of waiting in a crowded corridor, you arrive 15 minutes prior to your exact consultation window.

Do I need an advance booking, or can I walk in directly? +

Walk-in patients are warmly welcomed! Our Ground Floor Reception Desks (Counters 1 & 2) issue on-the-spot walk-in tokens for open slots. Advance online booking simply gives you priority slot selection and avoids unexpected chamber rush.

What should I do if I am experiencing a critical emergency? +

Do not wait for an OPD token. Call government emergency ambulance 108 or 112 immediately. (CarePulse Demo Hotline: 1800-000-0000). Note: this demo prototype does not dispatch actual ambulances.

Can I reschedule or cancel my booked appointment? +

Yes, completely free of charge. Open "My Tokens" from the navigation bar, view your digital token slip, and tap "🔄 Reschedule" to pick a new date/slot, or "❌ Cancel" to free your token for another patient in need.

Which cashless insurance policies and government cards are supported? +

CarePulse is empanelled with Star Health, HDFC ERGO, ICICI Lombard, Care Health, Medi Assist, Paramount TPA, and the Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY national scheme. Our on-site TPA coordinators arrange 15-minute pre-authorizations for IPD admissions.

How does CarePulse protect my medical data under the DPDP Act 2023? +

We strictly comply with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023. Patient UHID records and pathology lab results require OTP authentication before viewing, and medical history is never shared with third parties or advertisers.

Digital Health Data Privacy Policy

COMPLIANT WITH DIGITAL PERSONAL DATA PROTECTION (DPDP) ACT, 2023 (INDIA)

1. Principles of Data Collection

CarePulse Super Speciality Hospital collects patient identifiers (Name, Age, Gender, Mobile Number, Place of Residence, Symptoms, and ABHA ID) solely for clinical triage, OPD token queue scheduling, and verified pathology report generation.

2. OTP-Protected Diagnostic Reports

In accordance with clinical confidentiality standards, all digital diagnostic test sheets and pathology records are shielded behind mandatory 6-digit OTP verification sent to the patient's registered contact number.

3. Zero Commercial Monetization

We do not sell, license, or transmit your private health records to advertising networks or unverified third parties. Data is stored on secure, encrypted hospital servers situated within the territory of India.

4. Right to Correction and Erasure

Under Section 12 of the DPDP Act 2023, patients maintain the right to review, update, or request the closure of their digital portal profile by contacting the Hospital Data Protection Officer (DPO) at privacy@carepulsephagwara.org.
